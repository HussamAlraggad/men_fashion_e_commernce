# Men Fashion Online Store (E-commerse)

## The description of the project idea

I once thought of a similar store to one of the online stores (Women fur clothings) I made once, then I decided to build one for men but with some special details... I wanted the website (store) to look elegant, full of manhood, a store that only real men enter, AND the special addition to all of this is:
fitting room made special for and only for men. All you need to de there is either take a photo or upload it as an image file, then you will get a full body review for you to fit whatever outfit you want, all with the help of the AI in the fitting room.
